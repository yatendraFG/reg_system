DISCIPLINARY ACTION POLICY
Document ID: HR-DIS-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Purpose
Provide a fair framework for addressing misconduct or policy violations.

2. Examples
Examples may include repeated unauthorized absence, serious misconduct, harassment, fraud, data misuse, theft, or deliberate security violations.

3. Investigation
The company may review facts, documents, system records, and employee explanations before making a decision.

4. Corrective Actions
Depending on seriousness and applicable law, actions may include counseling, warning, performance/corrective plan, suspension where lawful, or termination.

5. Employee Response
Employees should be given an appropriate opportunity to explain relevant facts before final disciplinary decisions, subject to applicable procedure.

6. Confidentiality
Disciplinary information is restricted to authorized persons.
