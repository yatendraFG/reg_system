NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
INFORMATION SECURITY STANDARD
Document ID: IT-STD-002 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Access Principle
Access should follow least privilege and business need.

2. Authentication
MFA should be used for systems supporting it. Credentials must not be shared.

3. Encryption
Sensitive information should use approved encryption in transit and at rest where supported.

4. Backups
Critical systems should have scheduled backups and periodic recovery testing.

5. Vulnerability Management
Security updates and vulnerabilities should be prioritized based on risk.

6. Logging
Security-relevant events should be logged and reviewed according to system criticality.

7. Incident Response
Security incidents are identified, contained, investigated, remediated, and documented.

8. Third Parties
Third-party access must be approved and controlled.
