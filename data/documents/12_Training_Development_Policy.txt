NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT POLICY
Document ID: HR-LND-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Purpose
Support employees in building skills needed for current and future roles.

2. Training Types
Training may include technical courses, compliance training, workshops, mentoring, certifications, and leadership development.

3. Approval
External training requests require manager approval and may require HR/budget approval.

4. Mandatory Training
Security, privacy, workplace conduct, and other designated training must be completed within the assigned timeline.

5. Learning Records
HR may maintain training completion and certification records.

6. Knowledge Sharing
Employees are encouraged to share relevant learning through internal sessions and documentation.
