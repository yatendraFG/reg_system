NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Document ID: FIN-EXP-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Pre-Approval
Business travel should be approved before booking.

2. Transportation
Employees should use reasonable, cost-effective transportation consistent with role and business needs.

3. Accommodation
Hotels should be selected within approved company limits unless an exception is authorized.

4. Meals
Reasonable business travel meal expenses are reimbursable within applicable limits.

5. Receipts
Original or valid digital receipts should be retained and submitted with the expense claim.

6. Expense Submission
Claims should normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after completion of travel.

7. Non-Reimbursable
Personal entertainment, fines, unauthorized upgrades, and expenses without required approval are generally not reimbursable.

8. Exceptions
Exceptions require documented approval from the authorized manager/Finance.
