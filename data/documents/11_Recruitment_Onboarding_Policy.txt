NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
RECRUITMENT & ONBOARDING POLICY
Document ID: HR-REC-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Recruitment Principles
Recruitment should be merit-based, role-relevant, consistent, and compliant with applicable law.

2. Job Approval
New positions require approved headcount and budget before recruitment begins.

3. Selection
Candidates may undergo screening, interviews, technical/functional assessments, reference checks, and other lawful checks appropriate to the role.

4. Offer
Selected candidates receive written employment terms.

5. Onboarding
HR coordinates documentation, IT access, equipment, orientation, policy acknowledgements, and manager induction.

6. Background Verification
Where applicable, verification must be conducted with appropriate authorization and privacy safeguards.

7. Candidate Data
Candidate information must be handled only for legitimate recruitment purposes and protected from unauthorized access.
