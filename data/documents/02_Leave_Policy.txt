NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: HR-POL-002 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time employees receive:
- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per calendar year.
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per calendar year.
- Earned/Privilege Leave (EL): 18 days per calendar year.

2. Leave Application
Planned leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance through the leave system. Approval depends on workload and manager availability.

3. Emergency Leave
Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible. HR may request supporting information where permitted by law.

4. Sick Leave
Employees may be asked for reasonable medical documentation for extended or repeated absence, subject to applicable law and company practice.

5. Carry Forward
Up to 15 unused EL days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. CL and SL do not carry forward unless a specific statutory or company exception applies.

6. Leave Without Pay
Absence without available approved leave may be treated as leave without pay and may require HR review.

7. Holidays
Company holidays are published annually. Leave adjacent to holidays is still subject to approval.

8. Approval
Employees submit requests to their reporting manager. Extended leave may require HR approval.

9. Separation
Leave treatment at exit is handled according to the employment contract, applicable law, and payroll rules.
