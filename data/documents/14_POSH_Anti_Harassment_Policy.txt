NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
ANTI-HARASSMENT & POSH POLICY
Document ID: HR-POSH-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Zero Tolerance
The company prohibits sexual harassment and other unlawful workplace harassment.

2. Scope
The policy applies to employees, contractors, interns, visitors, and work-related interactions, subject to applicable law.

3. Reporting
A concern may be reported through the designated Internal Committee/authorized complaint channel or HR, as applicable.

4. Confidentiality
Complaint information should be handled confidentially, subject to legal and investigation requirements.

5. Non-Retaliation
Retaliation against a person making a good-faith complaint or participating in an investigation is prohibited.

6. Investigation
Complaints are handled through the process required by applicable law and company procedure.

7. Training
Relevant employees and committee members receive periodic awareness/training.
