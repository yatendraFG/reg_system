NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
CODE OF CONDUCT
Document ID: HR-ETH-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

Employees must act honestly, professionally, and in the company's legitimate interests.

1. Respectful Workplace
Bullying, harassment, threats, intimidation, and discriminatory conduct are prohibited.

2. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts to their manager or HR.

3. Gifts
Gifts or hospitality must not influence business decisions and must follow company approval thresholds.

4. Company Assets
Company property, information, software, and funds must be used responsibly.

5. Fraud
Fraud, falsification of records, bribery, theft, and deliberate misuse of company resources are prohibited.

6. Reporting
Concerns may be reported to a manager, HR, compliance contact, or designated reporting channel.

7. Non-Retaliation
Good-faith reporting should not result in retaliation.
