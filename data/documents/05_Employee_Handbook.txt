NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Document ID: HR-HBK-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

Welcome to NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. This handbook summarizes key workplace practices.

1. First Day
Employees complete onboarding, identity/payroll formalities, policy acknowledgements, and required security training.

2. Workplace Culture
Employees are expected to communicate respectfully, collaborate, meet commitments, and maintain professional conduct.

3. Manager Relationship
Employees should discuss goals, priorities, blockers, and feedback with their reporting manager.

4. Working Hours & Attendance
Standard hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM Monday-Friday unless the role has an approved shift.

5. Leave
Leave must be requested through the approved system and should be planned in advance where possible.

6. Technology
Company systems and data must be used according to IT and information security policies.

7. Confidential Information
Do not disclose customer, employee, product, financial, source-code, credential, or strategic information without authorization.

8. Performance
Performance is assessed against role expectations, goals, quality, collaboration, and conduct.

9. Learning
Employees are encouraged to participate in relevant training and maintain professional skills.

10. Exit
Employees must complete notice, handover, asset return, access closure, and exit formalities.
