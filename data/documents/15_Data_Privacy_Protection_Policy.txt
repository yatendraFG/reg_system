NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
DATA PRIVACY & PROTECTION POLICY
Document ID: LEG-PRIV-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Purpose
Protect personal and confidential information processed by the company.

2. Data Minimization
Collect and use only information reasonably required for legitimate business purposes.

3. Access
Access to personal data is restricted according to job responsibilities.

4. Storage
Personal and confidential data must be stored in approved systems with appropriate security controls.

5. Sharing
Data may be shared with authorized service providers or parties where there is a legitimate business/legal basis and appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention
Data is retained only as long as required by business, contractual, legal, or regulatory requirements.

7. Data Incident
Suspected loss, unauthorized disclosure, or compromise of personal data must be reported immediately to the designated security/privacy contact.

8. Individual Requests
Requests concerning personal information are handled through the company's designated privacy process.
