NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
ATTENDANCE & WORKING HOURS POLICY
Document ID: HR-ATT-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Standard Schedule
Normal working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM Monday-Friday with a one-hour meal break.

2. Attendance Recording
Employees must record attendance accurately using the approved system.

3. Late Arrival
Occasional delays should be communicated to the manager. Repeated unexplained lateness may require corrective action.

4. Early Departure
Employees should obtain manager approval when leaving during working hours for non-emergency reasons.

5. Overtime
Overtime, where applicable, must be pre-approved and is handled according to role terms and applicable law.

6. Shift Employees
Shift schedules may differ from standard hours and will be communicated by the relevant department.

7. Remote Employees
Remote employees must follow their agreed working schedule and attendance process.
