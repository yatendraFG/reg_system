NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
RESIGNATION & EXIT POLICY
Document ID: HR-EXIT-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Resignation
Employees must submit resignation through the designated process. Notice requirements are governed by the employment contract.

2. Notice Period
The standard notice period is 60 calendar days unless the appointment letter specifies another period or an approved exception applies.

3. Handover
Employees must complete knowledge transfer, documentation, task handover, and return company property.

4. Access Closure
IT disables or modifies system access according to the approved exit timeline.

5. Assets
All company assets must be returned before exit closure unless otherwise authorized.

6. Full & Final Settlement
HR and Finance process eligible salary, approved expenses, recoveries, leave treatment, and other dues according to applicable rules.

7. Experience/Relieving Documents
Eligible exit documents are issued after required clearance and processing.
