NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Document ID: HR-WFH-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Eligibility
WFH is role- and business-dependent and requires manager approval unless the role is designated remote.

2. Availability
Employees must remain reachable during agreed working hours and attend scheduled meetings.

3. Workspace
Employees should maintain a safe and reasonably private work environment.

4. Security
Company information must be accessed only through approved devices and systems. Sensitive discussions should not be conducted where unauthorized persons can hear them.

5. Equipment
Company-provided equipment remains company property and must be protected.

6. Performance
WFH does not change performance expectations or deliverables.

7. Revocation
WFH approval may be modified or withdrawn based on business requirements, performance, security, or operational needs.
