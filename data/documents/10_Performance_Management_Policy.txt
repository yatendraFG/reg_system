NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document ID: HR-PERF-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Objective
Create a transparent process for setting goals, feedback, development, and performance decisions.

2. Goal Setting
Managers and employees agree on measurable role-related goals.

3. Check-ins
Managers should provide periodic feedback and discuss progress, blockers, and support needs.

4. Annual Review
Formal reviews are generally conducted annually. Review outcomes may inform development and compensation decisions.

5. Performance Improvement Plan
Where performance materially falls below expectations, the company may use a documented improvement plan with objectives, support, review dates, and outcomes.

6. Promotion
Promotion depends on sustained performance, demonstrated capability, role availability, and business need.

7. Documentation
Managers should maintain objective records of goals and feedback.
