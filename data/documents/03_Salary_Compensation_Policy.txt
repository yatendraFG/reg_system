NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
SALARY & COMPENSATION POLICY
Document ID: FIN-HR-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Payroll Cycle
Salary is processed monthly and is targeted for credit on the last working day of each month, subject to bank and payroll processing.

2. Compensation Structure
Compensation may include basic salary, allowances, employer benefits, variable pay, incentives, and other components specified in the employment letter.

3. Deductions
Statutory deductions and authorized deductions are applied as required by law or written authorization.

4. Payslips
Employees can access monthly payslips through the designated HR/payroll system.

5. Salary Revision
Compensation reviews are generally conducted annually, but revision is not automatic and depends on performance, role, market conditions, and company performance.

6. Variable Pay
Eligibility, targets, calculation, and payment of incentives are governed by the applicable incentive plan.

7. Salary Confidentiality
Employees should handle payroll information responsibly and must not access another employee's payroll information without authorization.

8. Full & Final Settlement
At separation, final salary, eligible leave treatment, recoveries, and other dues are processed according to company procedures and applicable law.

9. Changes
Finance and HR jointly administer this policy.
