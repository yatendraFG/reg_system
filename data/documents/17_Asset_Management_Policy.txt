NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
ASSET MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document ID: IT-AM-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Asset Assignment
IT records company devices and other assigned assets against the responsible employee or department.

2. Employee Responsibility
Employees must protect assigned assets from loss, damage, unauthorized access, and misuse.

3. Software Licensing
Only authorized/licensed software may be installed on company assets.

4. Repairs
Hardware faults should be reported to IT rather than repaired through unauthorized vendors.

5. Lost/Stolen Assets
Report lost or stolen assets immediately so IT can take protective action.

6. Return
Assets must be returned during role change or separation as instructed.

7. Disposal
IT disposes of assets using approved processes, including secure data wiping where applicable.
