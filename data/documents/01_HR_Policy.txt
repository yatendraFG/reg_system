NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
HR POLICY
Document ID: HR-POL-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Purpose
This policy defines fair and consistent HR practices for employees and managers.

2. Employment
Employment is subject to the appointment letter, applicable law, company policies, and business requirements. Employees must provide accurate information during recruitment and onboarding.

3. Equal Opportunity
Employment decisions are based on qualifications, performance, role requirements, and business needs. Discrimination and retaliation are prohibited.

4. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour meal break. Business teams may have approved shift schedules.

5. Attendance
Employees must record attendance through the designated system. Repeated unexplained absence or late attendance may result in corrective action.

6. Probation
The standard probation period is six months unless the appointment letter specifies otherwise. Confirmation depends on performance, conduct, and business requirements.

7. Employee Records
Employees must keep contact, bank, tax, and emergency information current with HR.

8. Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company, customer, employee, and business information during and after employment.

9. Grievances
Employees may raise workplace concerns with their reporting manager or HR. Complaints will be handled confidentially to the extent practical.

10. Policy Administration
HR owns this policy and may revise it with management approval. In case of conflict, applicable law and the employment contract prevail.
