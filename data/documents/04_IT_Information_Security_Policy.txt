NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
IT & INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document ID: IT-SEC-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2026

1. Purpose
Protect company systems, devices, credentials, applications, and information.

2. Passwords
Passwords must be unique, confidential, and not shared. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled where provided.

3. Company Devices
Company laptops and devices are for authorized business use. Employees must apply security updates and use approved security software.

4. Software
Employees may install software only when authorized or available through approved company channels. Pirated or unlicensed software is prohibited.

5. Email & Internet
Company email and internet resources must be used responsibly. Phishing, malware, harassment, illegal activity, and unauthorized data transfer are prohibited.

6. Remote Access
Remote access must use approved VPN or security controls when required. Employees must avoid unsecured public networks for sensitive work.

7. Data Protection
Confidential data must be stored and transmitted using approved systems. Sensitive files should not be copied to personal drives or unauthorized cloud services.

8. Lost Device
Loss or theft of a company device must be reported to IT/security immediately. IT may remotely lock or wipe supported devices.

9. Security Incident
Suspected phishing, credential compromise, malware, unauthorized access, or data leakage must be reported immediately to the IT/security team.

10. Access Control
Access is granted based on job requirements and should be removed or changed when roles change or employment ends.
